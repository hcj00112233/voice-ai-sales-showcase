# 90-second demo script

A suggested narration and click path, not a recorded build-time or performance claim.

**0:00–0:15 | Load an account**

Click **Try a sample account**, choose **Lisan Learning**, and show the supplied notes.

“This is Voice AI Prospecting Workflow, an independent portfolio prototype. It turns supplied account research into outreach preparation. Lisan Learning is fictional, and this is explicitly labeled Sample output mode, using prewritten examples.”

**0:15–0:32 | Evidence and fit**

Click **Explore account fit**. Point to a quoted source, the hypothesis, and the unknowns.

“The notes describe English lessons and monthly content updates. That supports exploring the account. It does not establish budget or buying intent. Evidence is quoted directly; potential value is labeled as a hypothesis, and missing facts stay unknown.”

**0:32–0:48 | Use case and discovery**

Click **Next: Voice AI use case** and show the three questions.

“The idea is English lesson narration. Product or Learning Content is a suggested buyer function, not an invented contact. Discovery asks about production, language standards, and pilot success. A pilot would test quality and editing effort with human reviewers.”

**0:48–1:04 | Edit the outreach**

Click **Next: Outreach draft**. Change the subject to “A question about your English lesson workflow” and replace `[Your name]` with your own name. Click **Copy draft**.

“The email connects an observed workflow to one conditional idea and asks for a short conversation. I can edit and copy the current version. Nothing is sent from this demo.”

**1:04–1:30 | Approve and export**

Click **Next: Review & handoff**. Review the summary and tick all three checks. Click **Approve preparation package**, then **Markdown** or **JSON**.

“Approval means I reviewed these materials. Commercial status stays Discovery pending. The export includes my edited email and the open questions. Before approval it is labeled Unreviewed draft. If I change the research, results become outdated; if I edit the email, approval is removed. The workflow prepares a human sales decision.”
